    <div><div class="kicker">05 / Think through RG Luffy</div><h2>Count the defense they can buy.</h2></div>
    <p class="subtle">Then choose an attack and a defense plan. No line is always right.</p>
  </div>
  <p class="fine"><span class="tag">CARD RULE</span> The math below assumes an unmodified 5,000-power Luffy leader.</p>
  <div class="split">
    <article class="panel">
      <h3>Check the leader condition</h3>
      <p>Luffy needs DON!!×1 attached and five or fewer active DON!!. During each opponent's attack, Luffy may rest any number of active DON!! to gain +2,000 power for each one for that battle. Choose Luffy or one Straw Hat character. It can use the effect again in another battle. With more than five active DON!!, the effect is not available unless that count changes. DON!!×1 is a condition, not a payment.</p>
      <p>Against an unmodified 5,000-power Luffy:</p>
      <div class="math">5k or 6k attack → 1 rested DON!! can raise Luffy to 7k.<br />7k or 8k attack → 2 rested DON!! can raise Luffy to 9k.<br />Other power changes, counters, or taking the hit can change the result.</div>
    </article>
    <article class="panel">
      <h3>Compare wide and tall attacks</h3>
      <p>A 5k attack plus one DON!! becomes 6k. That does not ask Luffy to rest more DON!! than a 5k attack: one can still raise Luffy to 7k.</p>
      <p>A 5k attack plus two DON!! becomes 7k. Luffy may need to rest two DON!! to reach 9k instead of one. But Luffy can use a counter or take the hit instead. You are not forcing a DON!! spend.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
  <p class="fine spaced"><span class="tag">IDEAS TO TEST</span> The best attack depends on your life, board, and the DON!! still active.</p>
  <div class="decision-grid">
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You want several attacks</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> several attacks may ask Luffy to choose where to spend its remaining DON!!.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> small attacks may still be covered by the same power boost. You may get no extra DON!! value from adding only one DON!!.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> how many active DON!! did Luffy have for the next battle?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You want one big attack</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> two DON!! on a 5k attacker makes 7k and may ask for two rested DON!! to reach 9k against a 5k leader.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> DON!! spent on attack is not available for another play. Luffy can still counter or take the hit.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> did the larger attack change their choice or protect your board?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>Your Wisdom body may be KO'd</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> if Robin's once-per-turn life effect is still available. It only works when a Wisdom character without Blocker is KO'd—not when it leaves another way.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> taking a hit can risk life or lethal. Spending a counter can use a card that protects another turn.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> was the life gain worth the body, or was the body more useful alive?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>Choose an attack target</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> whether a character or leader attack helps more. Check the board before you spend DON!!.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> an attack at the leader may speed your clock; a character attack may reduce their next turn.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> which target gave you the better next turn?</p></article>
  </div>
