    <div><div class="kicker">04 / Think through Blue Rocks</div><h2>Plan for what you see.</h2></div>
    <p class="subtle">Treat non-KO interaction as a question to prepare for, not a claim that every Rocks list uses it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="callout"><span class="tag">CARD RULE</span> If a Wisdom body leaves without being KO'd, Robin's leader does not add life. <strong>Question:</strong> how much value did the body already give you, and what will you do if it leaves?</div>
  <div class="cards rocks-card-grid spaced">

      <p><b>Rule:</b> On Play, draw one. If one of your characters would be removed by an opponent's effect, Kyo can let you trash two cards from hand to stop it. This can cover KO, bottom-deck, or bounce effects. It does not replace a KO in battle.</p>
      <p><b>Choice:</b> is saving this body worth two cards from hand? Count Kyo's actual hand cost before calling a removal line.</p>

      <p><b>Rule:</b> with a Rocks Pirates leader, if John is rested, the opposing player can attack only Captain John. This restriction includes leader attacks. Its Main effect can rest John to draw one and trash one.</p>
      <p><b>Choice:</b> check John before Shanks. Shanks rests all opposing characters, which can rest John and turn on the attack restriction. Do not assume you can rest the board and then attack their leader.</p>

      <p><b>Choice:</b> it cannot target six-cost Nico Robin. It can target a five-cost Wisdom body and does not trigger Robin's KO life effect. This Blue card is an opposing threat, not a legal Robin deck suggestion.</p>

  <p class="fine"><span class="tag">IDEAS TO TEST</span> These are decisions to practice. They do not assume what every Rocks list will do.</p>
  <div class="decision-grid">
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>Your hand cannot reach the engine</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> use a search card for the missing part, not a card you already hold.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> the searcher uses a deck slot and may not answer the board.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> did the search find Robin or Nami, and did you have time to use it?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You expect a body may leave without a KO</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> whether its On Play already gave you enough value. Keep a second play ready if losing that body would stop your plan.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> holding a card can slow your board. Playing it now can give the opponent a useful target.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> what effect actually removed it? Did it KO, return, bottom-deck, or trash it?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You want to use Megalo</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> check for a rested opposing character at 8,000 power or less before playing Megalo. Your attacks do not normally rest opposing characters.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> four DON!! instead of keeping Megalo as a +2,000 counter.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> was there a legal target? Did removing it change the board or the clock?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You are thinking about Shanks</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> can the Rush attack close the game, or does resting their characters make your next turn safer? Shanks does not rest their leader.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> ten DON!! and a card that may be hard to use early. It rests characters; it does not remove them.</p><p><b>Choose:</b> spend one returned DON!! on Shanks or keep one active for OP14-118. The event needs two life or less and an active opposing character. You cannot play four-cost Megalo in the same normal 10-DON!! turn.</p></article>
  </div>
  <p class="fine">Do not assume a particular Rocks answer. Write down the card and effect you saw, then decide whether your plan should change.</p>
