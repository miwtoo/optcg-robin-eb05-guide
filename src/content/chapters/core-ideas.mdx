    <div><div class="kicker">02 / Card jobs and trade-offs</div><h2>Choose a job. Choose a card.</h2></div>
    <p class="subtle">These are options to think about, not required counts. Open a card to see what it adds, what it costs, and what to watch in testing.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="choice-grid">
    <ChoiceCard title="Find the right cards" subtitle="Atlas · Lilith" cards={[{ name: "Atlas", id: "EB05-050" }, { name: "Lilith", id: "OP13-113" }, { name: "Nico Robin", id: "EB05-016" }, { name: "Nami", id: "EB05-055" }]}>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> your opening hand cannot reach the part you need.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate cards:</b> Atlas costs one and looks at the top four for a Wisdom character. Lilith also costs one and looks at the top four for a Trigger card other than Lilith. Nico Robin and Nami fit both search rules.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> more looks at the engine pieces you need. <b>Give up:</b> a searcher takes a slot that could be defense or a threat, and can feel weak when you already have the pieces.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record what each search finds and what you still lack. <b>Change your mind if:</b> searches often miss the needed role, or you keep drawing searchers without a useful next play.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Build the Robin–Nami line" subtitle="Six-cost Robin · Nami" cards={[{ name: "Nico Robin", id: "EB05-016" }, { name: "Nami", id: "EB05-055" }]}>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> you need to build board and recover life.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate cards:</b> six-cost Nico Robin can play a cost-five-or-less Wisdom card from hand. Nami is a five-cost Wisdom card. On play, Nami can move the top card of your deck to the top of life when your leader has Wisdom.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> develop two characters in one turn, then add life. <b>Give up:</b> six DON!! and a Nami in hand. The line may be too slow when you need defense or a different board answer.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> note when Robin and Nami are both ready, and what threat you left unanswered. <b>Change your mind if:</b> the line keeps arriving late or does not help you survive the next turn.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Answer a rested body" subtitle="Megalo" cards={[{ name: "Megalo", id: "EB04-018" }]}>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> an opposing character is already rested and is at 8,000 power or less.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Megalo. On play, it may rest itself to KO one legal rested character.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> remove that target. <b>Give up:</b> four DON!! and a body with a +2,000 counter that you could have kept in hand. No legal target? The on-play removal does not help.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record how often a legal target exists when you draw Megalo. <b>Change your mind if:</b> it sits in hand as a counter, or the target is usually not rested.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Open a late attack window" subtitle="Shanks" cards={[{ name: "Shanks", id: "OP17-022" }]}>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> you need a large attacker and a turn with fewer opposing characters ready.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Shanks is ten cost, 12,000 power, and has Rush. On play, it reactivates up to two of your DON!!, then rests all opposing characters.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> it can attack that turn and may leave DON!! for a defensive event. It rests characters; it does not KO them or rest the opposing leader. <b>Give up:</b> an expensive card with no counter that may sit in hand early.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> did Shanks create a close, or did you need its slot earlier? <b>Change your mind if:</b> it is still in hand when you lose or there is no useful attack after it rests the board.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Add a blocker in a tight game" subtitle="Trafalgar Law" cards={[{ name: "Trafalgar Law", id: "OP13-031" }]}>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> you need a defensive body or another on-play character.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Law gains Blocker only when you have one life or less. On play, it may return one of your characters to hand; if you do, play up to one cost-five-or-less character from hand rested.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> a late Blocker or a way to reuse a useful body. <b>Give up:</b> six DON!!, a character returned from your field, and a new rested body. It has no printed counter.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record when the life condition is met and what body you returned. <b>Change your mind if:</b> the blocker condition arrives too late or returning a body slows you down.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Draw, gain life, or lower power" subtitle="Sweet 3 Generals" cards={[{ name: "Sweet 3 Generals", id: "OP17-114" }]}>
      <p><b>Problem:</b> your hand, life, or board needs help on your turn.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Sweet 3 Generals is a six-cost Slash/Strike character, not Wisdom, with a +1,000 counter. On your turn, its On Play may rest two DON!! to draw one, add up to one top card to life, then give up to two opposing characters −3,000 power for the turn.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> several useful effects in one card. <b>Give up:</b> two DON!! that cannot fund another play. Six-cost Robin cannot play it.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record whether the draw, life, or power drop changed the turn. <b>Change your mind if:</b> resting two DON!! leaves no useful follow-up.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
  </div>
  <p class="fine spaced">A package is a group of cards that solves one job. Add or remove a package to answer a problem you can name. Do not change several jobs at once and guess which change mattered.</p>
  <p class="fine"><strong>Track the card pool:</strong> how many Wisdom cards Atlas can find; how many Trigger cards Lilith can find; how often your hand has a useful 2,000 counter; and how often high-cost cards arrive before you can use them. Record search results from each game. Do not assume a certain count is best.</p>
