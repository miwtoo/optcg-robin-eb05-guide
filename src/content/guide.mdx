    <div><div class="kicker">01 / Why Robin</div><h2>Access, board, and life.</h2></div>
    <p class="subtle">A plan for turning a good hand into choices on later turns.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="callout">
    <strong>The working idea:</strong> one set of cards can link search, board, and life.
    Six-cost Nico Robin can bring Nami from hand. Nami can put the top card of your deck
    on top of your life. Once per turn, Robin's leader can do the same when a Wisdom
    character without Blocker is KO'd. This can help you keep playing—but costs turns
    and DON!!, and is not proof of a good matchup.
  </div>
  <div class="cards spaced">
    <article class="panel">
      <h3>What the numbers say</h3>
      <div class="stat"><b>52.8%</b><span>Robin overall · 62,464 games · 4,823 players</span></div>
      <p>Ranked, 7-day view, updated Nov 11, 2026.</p>
    </article>
    <article class="panel">
      <h3>What the numbers cannot say</h3>
      <p><strong>46%</strong> vs RG Luffy (7,707 games; first 46.4%, second 46.5%) and
      <strong>48%</strong> vs Rocks (6,040 games; first 47.9%, second 47.8%). The data
      does not control for the exact list, player, or event.</p>
      <p>Use these as reasons to practice—not as proof the matchups are bad or that one
      list is best.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
  <div class="split spaced">
    <article class="panel"><h3>Ask before a turn</h3><p>What do I need to do now: find a missing engine card, keep a useful body, answer a threat, or save DON!! for defense?</p></article>
    <article class="panel"><h3>Then name the cost</h3><p>Every choice uses something: DON!!, a card from hand, a turn of setup, or space for another answer. A strong plan sees both the gain and the cost.</p></article>
  </div>

      <p><b>Problem:</b> your opening hand cannot reach the part you need.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate cards:</b> Atlas costs one and looks at the top four for a Wisdom character. Lilith also costs one and looks at the top four for a Trigger card other than Lilith. Nico Robin and Nami fit both search rules.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> more looks at the engine pieces you need. <b>Give up:</b> a searcher takes a slot that could be defense or a threat, and can feel weak when you already have the pieces.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record what each search finds and what you still lack. <b>Change your mind if:</b> searches often miss the needed role, or you keep drawing searchers without a useful next play.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>

      <p><b>Problem:</b> you need to build board and recover life.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate cards:</b> six-cost Nico Robin can play a cost-five-or-less Wisdom card from hand. Nami is a five-cost Wisdom card. On play, Nami can move the top card of your deck to the top of life when your leader has Wisdom.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> develop two characters in one turn, then add life. <b>Give up:</b> six DON!! and a Nami in hand. The line may be too slow when you need defense or a different board answer.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> note when Robin and Nami are both ready, and what threat you left unanswered. <b>Change your mind if:</b> the line keeps arriving late or does not help you survive the next turn.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Answer a rested body" subtitle="Megalo">
      <p><b>Problem:</b> an opposing character is already rested and is at 8,000 power or less.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Megalo. On play, it may rest itself to KO one legal rested character.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> remove that target. <b>Give up:</b> four DON!! and a body with a +2,000 counter that you could have kept in hand. No legal target? The on-play removal does not help.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record how often a legal target exists when you draw Megalo. <b>Change your mind if:</b> it sits in hand as a counter, or the target is usually not rested.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Open a late attack window" subtitle="Shanks">
      <p><b>Problem:</b> you need a large attacker and a turn with fewer opposing characters ready.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Shanks is ten cost, 12,000 power, and has Rush. On play, it reactivates up to two of your DON!!, then rests all opposing characters.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> it can attack that turn and may leave DON!! for a defensive event. It rests characters; it does not KO them or rest the opposing leader. <b>Give up:</b> an expensive card with no counter that may sit in hand early.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> did Shanks create a close, or did you need its slot earlier? <b>Change your mind if:</b> it is still in hand when you lose or there is no useful attack after it rests the board.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Add a blocker in a tight game" subtitle="Trafalgar Law">

      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Law gains Blocker only when you have one life or less. On play, it may return one of your characters to hand; if you do, play up to one cost-five-or-less character from hand rested.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> a late Blocker or a way to reuse a useful body. <b>Give up:</b> six DON!!, a character returned from your field, and a new rested body. It has no printed counter.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record when the life condition is met and what body you returned. <b>Change your mind if:</b> the blocker condition arrives too late or returning a body slows you down.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
    <ChoiceCard title="Draw, gain life, or lower power" subtitle="Sweet 3 Generals">
      <p><b>Problem:</b> your hand, life, or board needs help on your turn.</p>
      <p><b>Candidate card:</b> Sweet 3 Generals is a six-cost Slash/Strike character, not Wisdom, with a +1,000 counter. On your turn, its On Play may rest two DON!! to draw one, add up to one top card to life, then give up to two opposing characters −3,000 power for the turn.</p>
      <p><b>Gain:</b> several useful effects in one card. <b>Give up:</b> two DON!! that cannot fund another play. Six-cost Robin cannot play it.</p>
      <p><b>Test:</b> record whether the draw, life, or power drop changed the turn. <b>Change your mind if:</b> resting two DON!! leaves no useful follow-up.</p>
    </ChoiceCard>
  </div>
  <p class="fine spaced">A package is a group of cards that solves one job. Add or remove a package to answer a problem you can name. Do not change several jobs at once and guess which change mattered.</p>
  <p class="fine"><strong>Track the card pool:</strong> how many Wisdom cards Atlas can find; how many Trigger cards Lilith can find; how often your hand has a useful 2,000 counter; and how often high-cost cards arrive before you can use them. Record search results from each game. Do not assume a certain count is best.</p>

      <p><b>Choice:</b> it cannot target six-cost Nico Robin. It can target a five-cost Wisdom body and does not trigger Robin's KO life effect. This Blue card is an opposing threat, not a legal Robin deck suggestion.</p>
      <a href="https://onepiece.limitlesstcg.com/cards/OP17-046" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Card text ↗</a>
    </article>
  </div>
  <p class="fine"><span class="tag">IDEAS TO TEST</span> These are decisions to practice. They do not assume what every Rocks list will do.</p>
  <div class="decision-grid">
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>Your hand cannot reach the engine</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> use a search card for the missing part, not a card you already hold.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> the searcher uses a deck slot and may not answer the board.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> did the search find Robin or Nami, and did you have time to use it?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You expect a body may leave without a KO</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> whether its On Play already gave you enough value. Keep a second play ready if losing that body would stop your plan.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> holding a card can slow your board. Playing it now can give the opponent a useful target.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> what effect actually removed it? Did it KO, return, bottom-deck, or trash it?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You want to use Megalo</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> attack first if that can leave a useful opposing character rested. Megalo can only KO a rested character at 8,000 power or less.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> four DON!! instead of keeping Megalo as a +2,000 counter.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> was there a legal target? Did removing it change the board or the clock?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You are thinking about Shanks</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> can the Rush attack close the game, or does resting their characters make your next turn safer? The leader stays active.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> ten DON!! and a card that may be hard to use early. It rests characters; it does not remove them.</p><p><b>Choose:</b> spend one returned DON!! on Shanks or keep one active for OP14-118. The event needs two life or less and an active opposing character. You cannot play four-cost Megalo in the same normal 10-DON!! turn.</p></article>
  </div>
  <p class="fine">Do not assume a particular Rocks answer. Write down the card and effect you saw, then decide whether your plan should change.</p>

<section class="section" id="luffy">
  <div class="section-head">
    <div><div class="kicker">05 / Think through RG Luffy</div><h2>Count the defense they can buy.</h2></div>
    <p class="subtle">Then choose an attack and a defense plan. No line is always right.</p>
  </div>
  <p class="fine"><span class="tag">CARD RULE</span> The math below assumes an unmodified 5,000-power Luffy leader.</p>
  <div class="split">
    <article class="panel">
      <h3>Check the leader condition</h3>
      <p>Luffy needs DON!!×1 attached and five or fewer active DON!!. During each opponent's attack, Luffy may rest any number of active DON!! to gain +2,000 power for each one for that battle. Choose Luffy or one Straw Hat character. It can use the effect again in another battle. With more than five active DON!!, the effect is not available unless that count changes. DON!!×1 is a condition, not a payment.</p>
      <p>Against an unmodified 5,000-power Luffy:</p>
      <div class="math">5k or 6k attack → 1 rested DON!! can raise Luffy to 7k.<br />7k or 8k attack → 2 rested DON!! can raise Luffy to 9k.<br />Other power changes, counters, or taking the hit can change the result.</div>
    </article>
    <article class="panel">
      <h3>Compare wide and tall attacks</h3>
      <p>A 5k attack plus one DON!! becomes 6k. That does not ask Luffy to rest more DON!! than a 5k attack: one can still raise Luffy to 7k.</p>
      <p>A 5k attack plus two DON!! becomes 7k. Luffy may need to rest two DON!! to reach 9k instead of one. But Luffy can use a counter or take the hit instead. You are not forcing a DON!! spend.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
  <p class="fine spaced"><span class="tag">IDEAS TO TEST</span> The best attack depends on your life, board, and the DON!! still active.</p>
  <div class="decision-grid">
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You want several attacks</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> several attacks may ask Luffy to choose where to spend its remaining DON!!.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> small attacks may still be covered by the same power boost. You may get no extra DON!! value from adding only one DON!!.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> how many active DON!! did Luffy have for the next battle?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>You want one big attack</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> two DON!! on a 5k attacker makes 7k and may ask for two rested DON!! to reach 9k against a 5k leader.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> DON!! spent on attack is not available for another play. Luffy can still counter or take the hit.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> did the larger attack change their choice or protect your board?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>Your Wisdom body may be KO'd</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> if Robin's once-per-turn life effect is still available. It only works when a Wisdom character without Blocker is KO'd—not when it leaves another way.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> taking a hit can risk life or lethal. Spending a counter can use a card that protects another turn.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> was the life gain worth the body, or was the body more useful alive?</p></article>
    <article class="decision"><span class="tag">IF</span><h3>Choose an attack target</h3><p><b>Consider:</b> whether a character or leader attack helps more. Check the board before you spend DON!!.</p><p><b>Cost:</b> an attack at the leader may speed your clock; a character attack may reduce their next turn.</p><p><b>Check next turn:</b> which target gave you the better next turn?</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section" id="rules">
  <div class="section-head">
    <div><div class="kicker">08 / Quick rules check</div><h2>Follow the card text.</h2></div>
    <p class="subtle">Check the card text in the language and version you use. These reminders do not replace a judge's ruling.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cards">
    <article class="panel"><h3>Robin leader</h3><p>Once per turn, when your Wisdom character <strong>without Blocker</strong> is KO'd, you may put the top card of your deck on top of your life. This effect does not work if the character has Blocker. It also does not work if an effect returns it to hand, puts it under the deck, or trashes it.</p></article>
    <article class="panel"><h3>Restand cap</h3><p>Once per turn, set one rested Wisdom character with current power 6,000 or less as active. Check its current power when you use this effect. An earlier power increase can put it over 6,000. If a DON!! would put it over the limit, set it as active first. You can attack again only if it can legally attack. This effect does not let a character attack on the turn it enters play.</p></article>
    <article class="panel"><h3>Six-cost Nico Robin</h3><p><strong>[Your Turn] [On Play]</strong> Play up to one <strong>Wisdom</strong> character that costs 5 or less from your hand. Choose a card that meets this limit.</p></article>
    <article class="panel"><h3>Megalo</h3><p>When you play Megalo, you may rest it to KO one opposing rested character with 8,000 power or less. The target must already be rested. You cannot use this effect again by setting Megalo as active. Megalo cannot attack on the turn it enters play unless an effect allows it.</p></article>
    <article class="panel"><h3>Shanks</h3><p>OP17-022 is a 10-cost, 12k Rush character. When you play it, set up to two of your DON!! as active. Then rest all opposing characters. You can save the active DON!! for a defensive event. Check your DON!! before you plan to play Shanks and a 4-cost Megalo in one turn.</p></article>
    <article class="panel"><h3>Law / event / Sweet 3</h3><p>OP13-031 Law has Blocker only when you have one life or less. On play, it may return one of your characters to your hand. Then you may play a character that costs 5 or less from your hand, rested. OP14-118 Counter works only when you have two life or less and an active opposing character. Sweet 3 Generals is six-cost, not Wisdom, and its On Play rests two DON!!.</p></article>
  </div>
  <div class="callout spaced"><strong>Before you play Shanks:</strong> check which opposing characters it can rest. Decide what you will do next. Resting a character does not remove it. Before you use Megalo, check that you can play it and that its target is already rested and has 8,000 power or less. Setting Megalo as active does not repeat its On Play effect.</div>

<section class="section" id="testing">
  <div class="section-head">
    <div><div class="kicker">09 / Test one idea</div><h2>Change your mind with evidence.</h2></div>
    <p class="subtle">Twenty games per matchup can show a pattern. They cannot prove a win rate.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="cards">
    <article class="panel"><h3>Run a fair comparison</h3><ol class="list"><li>Name one problem: missed engine, weak defense, dead Megalo, or Shanks stuck in hand.</li><li>Write a prediction. Example: “More search will help me find Robin and Nami.”</li><li>Change one package. Keep the rest steady for this comparison—not forever.</li><li>Play both first and second. Use the same opponent and list when you can.</li><li>Compare results, then decide whether to keep, undo, or test another change.</li></ol></article>
    <article class="panel"><h3>Ask what would change your mind</h3><ul class="list"><li>Did search miss the card you needed, or did you find it too late?</li><li>Did you lose with Robin and Nami ready, or because defense came up short?</li><li>Was Megalo dead because there was no rested target? Was Shanks dead because you could not reach ten DON!!?</li><li>Did the Luffy player rest DON!!, use counter, or take the hit?</li></ul></article>
  </div>
  <p class="callout spaced"><strong>Record causes, not just wins.</strong> Separate missed engine, unused cards, removal that did not KO, and defense choices. Ten games first and ten second is a useful diagnostic split, not proof.</p>
